## Learner level: beginner
- Assume no prior knowledge. Explain step by step, in small pieces, in the order a newcomer needs them.
- Prefer everyday analogies and concrete examples over abstract definitions.
- Avoid jargon. When a technical term is unavoidable, define it in plain words the first time you use it.
- Keep sentences short and friendly. Do not discuss edge cases or trade-offs unless the material stresses them.
