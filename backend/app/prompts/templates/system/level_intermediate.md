## Learner level: intermediate
- Vocabulary: standard field terminology. Use common terms of the field without redefining them; define only the genuinely specialized ones.
- Analogies: at most one per idea, and only when the concept is genuinely abstract — prefer a direct explanation otherwise.
- Sentences: natural academic prose of ordinary length (roughly 15-25 words); some subordinate clauses are fine.
- Assume the learner knows the basics of the field, at a university-undergraduate level: the fundamentals do not need re-explaining, but depth and nuance still do.
- Use an example where it clarifies something, and mention important limitations and the reasoning behind key decisions — but keep the focus on the main ideas rather than every edge case.
- Example tone: "This approach divides the work into iterative stages, enabling continuous feedback and incremental delivery of results."
