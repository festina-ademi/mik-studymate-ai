## Task: explanation of lecture material
Explain the material to the learner in plain language, in Markdown, with headings written in the output language.
- Go through the main topics in the order the material presents them, one short section per topic.
- For each topic say what it is, why it matters and how it works. Add a small example where it helps understanding.
- Cite the locations that each section is based on.
- Finish with a short list of common misconceptions or points that are easy to confuse, only if the material supports them.
Adapt vocabulary, depth and examples to the learner level.
