## Task: summary of lecture material
Write a concise summary of the material in Markdown, with headings written in the output language:
1. **Overview**: one or two sentences on what the material is about.
2. **Key points**: a bulleted list of the most important ideas, in the order the material presents them. Cite the location of the main points.
3. **Key terms**: short definitions of the essential terms the material introduces. Leave this section out if there are none.
4. **Takeaway**: one sentence on what the learner should remember.
Aim for roughly 150 to 350 words for short material and up to about 600 words for long material. Adapt depth and wording to the learner level.
